*About this experiment

A portfolio for experimentation, connected ideas, and the perspective of its creator, Sami Belina. The website itself is part of the inquiry.

The idea

A portfolio for experimentation, connecting ideas, and sharing its creator’s perspective. The website itself is part of that inquiry.

The working hypothesis

If a multidisciplinary approach is applied to website development, and the creator understands the language and methods being combined, can prompting a large language model produce new forms of web experience and communication?

The site tests the boundaries of what a website is expected to be and what it can become, while keeping its meaning accessible through reading, keyboard use and static alternatives. The question is whether that approach produces a meaningfully different experience from prompting for familiar page layouts. That remains an open question, not a demonstrated result.

The process

My writing, research, and observations inform the design; agents suggest structures and connections; I review their interpretation. We build small examples, inspect behavior, record sources and uncertainties, and revise.

The implementation

Built with React, TypeScript, and Vite for a Cloudflare Worker, with LLM-assisted development. This version includes reading templates, versioned content contracts, and explained connections. An isolated local writing trial has been exercised with synthetic manuscripts; hosted CMS integration and publication of new writing remain unimplemented. Original manuscripts stay separate from suggested edits. Automated checks provide technical evidence; human reading and accessibility review are part of the continuing work.

The interactive reading experiments ask whether making assumptions, evidence, and relationships inspectable helps visitors understand how I investigate systems. We record interface observations as the site evolves. Improved comprehension has not been established; technical checks are not a substitute for evaluating it with readers.

Reveal the hidden work

Does retaining a simple process while revealing the work beneath it help visitors inspect what the overview leaves out? This process is illustrative, not case evidence. Its lines encode relationships, not measured duration.

Technical observations: one reveal opens the extra steps in place; the original track remains visible; HTML and SVG contain the explanation without animation. Optional p5.js motion pauses offscreen and supports pause/reset and reduced motion. These are interface checks, not evidence of improved comprehension. Reader evaluation remains to be done.
