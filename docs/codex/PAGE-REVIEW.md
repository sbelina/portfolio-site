## What you review before approving

- The complete text and your contribution/credit.
- Title, description, URL, and page layout.
- Sources, qualifications, media rights, and accessibility.
- Connections and the reason each one appears.
- The exact previewed revision and the scope of the approval.

Checking a box in a Markdown worksheet is not a secure publishing mechanism. The editor must record the authenticated approval and publication separately, as described in [the workflow specification](WRITING-REVIEW-WORKFLOW.md).
