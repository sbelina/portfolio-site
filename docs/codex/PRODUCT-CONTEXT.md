# Product and interaction context

## What the site should make legible

Producing Curiosity presents Sam's work as an investigative observatory and field notebook. The organizing direction is **Seeing What Systems Miss**; **Things That Didn't Add Up** is a strong editorial framing. A visitor should come away thinking, “This person sees patterns I would have missed,” and understand how Sam helps institutions notice when assumptions, metrics, categories, and practices stop reflecting people's realities.

Lead with questions and reasoning rather than a gallery of deliverables or a list of UX methods. Show the movement from curiosity through evidence to a decision or durable structure. Preserve work that remains exploratory without pretending it produced an implemented business outcome.

The mycelium metaphor means connected context: work, ideas, observations, and writing can share meaningful relationships. It does not require a literal mushroom aesthetic or an interface that visitors must decode. A conventional reading route and a richer exploration route should expose the same underlying content.

## Visitor jobs

| Visitor | What they need | Useful first path |

| Recruiter or hiring manager | Understand role, contribution, scope, judgment, and relevance quickly. | Home → selected investigation → About/contact. |
| Research/design peer | Inspect evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, and cross-case ideas. | Investigation → evidence → explained pattern → another case. |
| Collaborator | Understand interests, approach, and a concrete way to engage. | Perspective or experiment → related work → contact. |
| Returning reader | Find what changed or resume a known piece. | Stable URL, archive/filter state, updated material. |
| Sam as author | Add and connect material without coding; know what is saved versus live. | Content studio → draft → private preview → controlled publication. |

Test these as hypotheses with representative people. Avoid making every visitor choose an audience persona before entering.

## Repeatable reading anatomy

An investigation generally moves through question/curiosity → assumed versus observed → evidence → consequence → response → what persisted. It need not force every case into the same number of modules.

Keep the thesis, short orientation, contribution, and material limitation easy to find. Let details unfold through readable sections, anchors, optional evidence disclosures, and contextual links. A pattern page is a proposition with examples and boundaries, not merely a tag landing page.

Provide a stable shell while allowing diagrams, linked concepts, or selected interactions to extend beyond the usual column when they aid comprehension. The intended feel is stable to read, alive to discover, structured to trust, and organic enough to feel personal.

## Visual semantics and behavior

| Signal | Meaning | Required companion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Violet | Unresolved question or evidence not yet understood. | Label, context, or shape. |
| Coral | Pressure, friction, defect, or a need to change. | Explicit description of the pressure. |
| Teal | Structure, what holds, what remained. | Evidence or a clearly qualified interpretation. |
| Reflective depth / temporal echo | Consequence of interaction or continuity across a transition. | Stable semantic controls and reduced-motion equivalent. |
| Shimmer or motion | A specific focus, activity, or state change. | Accurate status; no perpetual false loading. |

Preserve the existing intro's authored rhythm as documented in `../INTRO-STATES.md`, while allowing direct article access and a reliable skip. Keep meaningful content in the semantic DOM; decorative echoes remain hidden from assistive technology and non-interactive.

## Four connected lines of exploration

1. **Nested information and semantic zoom.** Let the same subject support overview, argument, and evidence. Decide explicitly which changes deserve a route, query, fragment, or local state. Keep Back/Forward and focus behavior predictable.
2. **Knowledge relationships.** Give edges a type, direction where applicable, rationale, provenance, and review status. Offer local views and text lists before a giant global graph. A visual line is not itself an explanation.
3. **Sensory meaning.** Use color, depth, movement, sound only if appropriate, and spatial placement as consistent feedback. Meaning must survive without animation, color perception, hover, or audio.
4. **Performance and experimentation.** Load enhancements progressively. Evaluate whether visitors understand the work and connections; do not equate time spent or click volume with comprehension. Keep prototypes labelled and separate observed results from simulations.

## Boundaries

The first release does not need a force-directed graph, automatic AI associations, real-time collaboration, vector search, or a generic visual page builder. It needs useful content, reliable routes, understandable relationships, and an authoring workflow that truly persists.

The hexagonal keyboard investigation remains separately scoped by [its research brief](../KEYBOARD-RESEARCH.md). Do not repeat the disputed “QWERTY was designed to slow typists” story as settled history. No ergonomic or speed superiority is established by an on-screen geometry comparison. It must not block the publishing milestone.

## Owner positioning and study areas — 2026-10-23

Homepage lead: “Across environments, I keep finding the same thing: the problem people see is rarely the one creating the outcome.”

Areas of inquiry: structural causality, information flow, incentives, feedback loops, hidden work, and organizational learning. These describe what Sam studies; they are not claims of proven causal effects. Connect future investigations to these areas only where the material supports the connection.

The intro requires one deliberate action total. Its three teaching stages unfold automatically, followed by the dive. Do not restore repeated-input gates: the owner wants the same teaching with less frustration and waiting. Whether this improves comprehension remains a question for observation, not an established result.
