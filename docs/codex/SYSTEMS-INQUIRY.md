## Two objects of inquiry

The **object system** comprises the people, information, resources, constraints, workflows, feedback and events that may generate observed behavior. The **inquiry system** comprises the observer's categories, instruments, incentives, assumptions, exclusions and decisions that make an explanation plausible.

Ask **“What way of looking at this makes it appear true?”** of both. Keep source → claim → interpretation → model → visualization → decision traceable. Changing a frame changes salience and permissible inference, not the evidence or the historical world. An interface may change attention without validating the mechanism it depicts. Treat every framework, including this charter, as revisable.

## Recurring inquiry loop

| Move | Subject-matter question | Self-application to development |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notice | What discrepancy was observed or reported, by whom and with what limits? | What actual reader/author problem or defect was observed, versus assumed? |
| Frame | Which unit, categories, actor position, goals, measures and boundary make an explanation plausible? | Whose reading needs and definition of success shape this design? |
| Model | What smallest mechanism could explain it? What alternatives predict something different? | Why might this representation help, and why might a paragraph suffice? |
| Test | Which observation would distinguish or disconfirm those accounts? | Which technical checks and human tasks test distinct assumptions? |
| Interrogate | Which actors, histories, delays, constraints, disabilities or contrary cases were omitted? | What do the interaction, schema and available tooling hide or privilege? |
| Revise | What changed, why, who reviewed it, and what remains uncertain? | Retain correction provenance; reduce or abandon a failed design hypothesis. |

These are logical moves, not required page sections or onboarding actions. Record decisions and observable evidence, not hidden deliberation. After revision, distinguish proposed action, Sam's contribution, actual adoption, measured consequences and persistence. Inspect operational/physical, informational and interpretive/social structures without treating those categories as exhaustive.

## Relationships, behavior and epistemic status

A **theory of relationships** maps dependencies, influence, association, information exchange or feedback. A **theory of behavior** additionally declares sufficient dynamics for testable predictions: variables/stocks/flows, units, actor rules, rates, delays, initial conditions, parameters, constraints and time scale. Arrows or animated paths alone supply none of these. Do not manufacture dynamics from static results.

Keep four layers explicit in prose and any future compatible data extension: **observed** (source/report/measurement), **interpreted** (bounded meaning), **modeled** (proposed mechanism), **simulated** (computed consequences of declared assumptions). “Documented” relationship status is not causal proof; editorial approval is not epistemic certainty. “Illustrative” is not “calibrated.” A proxy is not necessarily the phenomenon.

## Model integrity and challenge

For explanatory models proposed for public presentation, provide four inspectable properties, initially in concise prose through existing content blocks:

1. **Provenance:** stable source/claim references, observer/reporter, relevant dates/definitions, rights and review state. Missing information stays explicit; private sources remain private.
2. **Alternatives:** a plausible competitor or an explicit “meaningful alternatives not yet identified,” with discriminating evidence when available.
3. **Boundary conditions:** context, population, period, missing variables and where the account may fail; state when causation is unestablished.
4. **Revision record:** version/date, earlier explanation, challenge, change, reviewer and uncertainty. “No model revision recorded yet” is valid; never invent a narrative transformation.

These are adopted editorial requirements, **not claims that current Zod schemas enforce them**. Existing approved revisions are not silently rewritten. Review new explanatory candidates against them, using the existing exact-candidate approval boundary. Meaningful schema changes remain versioned, validated work.

Perform a **Model Challenger** review: what could make this false, which proxy is mistaken for an outcome, do labels embed the conclusion, what is excluded, and what competing mechanism fits the same evidence? Preserve uncertainties and reviewer identity honestly. A challenger can propose corrections and cannot approve its own publication.

## Bounded orchestration

For substantial work, assign relevant available specialists explicit ownership: research/epistemic critique; systems/information architecture; interaction/accessibility; content/provenance; experiment/quality; security/release. Use sequential passes if agents are unavailable and label them accordingly; do not claim independent review of one's own output.

Each pass supplies **question → proposed action → supporting evidence → risks/unknowns → validation method**. The integration lead reconciles conflicts and retains unresolved objections. A bounded task need not activate every specialist. Existing instructions govern privacy, authorization and publication.

Use Bildung (revision), Phronesis (situated judgment), Ma (intervals), Welterschließung (what a frame reveals) and Befindlichkeit (situated attention) as optional prompts. They are neither interchangeable authorities nor a validated synthesis; visitors need not learn their terminology.

## Instruments and transferable principles

Choose the instrument by the question: depth viewer, actor/relationship map, frame comparison, behavior explorer, revision record, or connection atlas. Before building, state what a visitor could answer beyond a clear paragraph or table. Prefer the smallest accessible representation; keep the stable reading path and exploration optional.

Patterns are bounded transferable propositions supported by specific reviewed cases, not extra empirical investigations or universal laws. Preserve case identities, contrary examples, scope and evidence that could force revision. A thematic link is not causation. Do not infer new relationships from tags, visual proximity or a candidate title.

The **Frame Laboratory remains a proposed bounded experiment**. Hold immutable evidence references/revisions constant; expose each frame's question, emphasis, permissible and unsupported conclusions, omissions, competitors and next test. Do not equate frames' evidentiary support. Compare against the same material in static text before claiming value. See [the assessment and acceptance gates](SYSTEMS-INQUIRY-ASSESSMENT.md) before any implementation.
