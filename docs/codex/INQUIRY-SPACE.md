It is nondirectional. Editorial acceptance does not upgrade its epistemic level. Supported pairings additionally require selected evidence and an investigation/pattern endpoint. Structural checks cannot determine source relevance or the truth of prose. Reviewed source/rights and exact owner approval remain necessary for real work.

The public projection allowlists these fields and binds them to selected record digests.
